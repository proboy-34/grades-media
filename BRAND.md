## The core idea: the 8-month system
- Play Group → Class 8 = 11 classes, each finished in **8 months** instead of 12.
- Classes 9 & 10 (Matric / O-Levels) run at normal length.
- Child joining at 3.5 years: finishes Class 8 at **10 y 10 m** (vs 14.5) and O-Levels at **12 y 10 m** (vs 16.5).
- **Saves 3 years 8 months** (11 classes × 4 months).
- Pitch for the saved time: coding, graphic design, video editing & content creation, digital marketing, freelancing, foreign languages, entrepreneurship.
- Taglines: "Har class sirf 8 mahine mein" · "Wohi Cambridge syllabus" · "You save 3 years 8 months"
